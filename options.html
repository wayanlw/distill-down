Clipboard

Copies one combined Markdown text (Cleaned). Always Markdown.

Links and images in text

Off keeps the link text and drops the address; images become [Image: description]. In a PDF, off also means no pictures are embedded. Does not affect Page print.

Download

Not used for the clipboard, which is always Cleaned. Page print shows a brief "debugging this browser" banner. Cleaned PDF supports Western European characters only; others print as "?". Use Page print for other scripts or mathematical notation.

Pages are converted in your browser and are never sent to the developer or an AI service. A PDF with images downloads those images from the sites that host them.
