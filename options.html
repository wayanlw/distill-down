Distill Down

Tabs in, clean text out. Do distill.

  • One setup, then one click. Choose your settings once. After that, every capture is a single click on the toolbar icon.
  • Many tabs at once. Select several tabs (Ctrl or Shift and click), then click once. They become one combined document.

Clipboard

Page text on your clipboard, ready to paste into an AI chat.

Marks where the page starts and ends, so an AI can tell it from your instructions.

Off: images show as [Image: description]. On: address kept too.

Off cuts everything after a ? or #, which can hold private details. Some sites (for example YouTube) need it for the link to work.
