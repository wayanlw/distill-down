# Distill Down privacy policy

Effective: 01/11/2026

Distill Down does not collect, store on a server, sell or share your data. It has no account, no analytics and no tracking. The developer receives nothing from your use of the extension.

## What the extension reads
When you click the toolbar icon, Distill Down reads the content of the tabs you have highlighted. It converts that content to Markdown on your device. The result goes only where you chose: your clipboard, a file in your Downloads folder, or both.

## What it stores
Your settings (for example clipboard on or off, destination folder) are saved on this device with Chrome's extension storage. Page content is never stored by the extension, other than in the files you ask it to save.

## Network requests
Distill Down makes no network requests at all. It contacts no AI service, analytics service or server run by the developer, and it does not download images or anything else. Conversion happens on your device.

## What goes into your output
By default, the source address in clipboard text and files has its query string and fragment removed, because they can hold tokens or private identifiers. You can turn on the full address in settings. Whatever you paste or upload elsewhere is then subject to that service's own terms.

## Permissions, and why
| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Tabs | Find the highlighted tabs and read their titles and addresses. |
| Scripting and access to all sites | Read each page's content so it can be cleaned and converted. Distill Down acts only on tabs you highlight and click for. |
| Downloads | Save the Markdown files you ask for. |
| Clipboard write | Copy the combined Markdown. |
| Storage | Remember your settings. |
| Notifications | Tell you when a capture finishes or a tab was skipped. |
| Offscreen documents | Copy to the clipboard and create files in a hidden page, as Chrome requires. |
